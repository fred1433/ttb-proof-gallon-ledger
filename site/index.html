Checks and assumptions

The form totals prove the draft adds up. The other checks compare it with things the ledger did not produce: last month as filed, the plant's register of records, the consignor's transfer record, the month-end count, and the same month worked out by hand. That is how an unreviewed gauge sheet shows up, and why a balanced draft is not yet a correct one.

    Assumptions to confirm with your TTB reviewer

      Not in this month

      • Production account and TTB F 5110.40: no distilling in this scenario. That is a limit of the demo, not a view of what your plant needs.
      • TTB F 5000.24 and any tax figure; Kentucky reports; exports; imported, Puerto Rican or Virgin Islands spirits.
      • Bottled spirits moving in bond (allowed in some cases since the CBMA changes): every removal here is tax determined, because its record says so.
      • Alternating proprietorship: one operator's report never reads another's entries, and a test proves it, but the workflow itself is not built.
      • Raw gauge readings (hydrometer, Tables 1, 4 and 7, obscuration): the gauge sheet starts from quantities the gauger already determined.
      • DistillX5 or Whiskey Systems imports, PDF export, users and permissions, storage, filing. A small generic CSV migration lives in the repository's tests.

      TypeScript with no runtime dependency; exact decimal arithmetic on integers, never floats; an append-only ledger where every entry sums to zero; tests on rounding, conversions, reversals, separation between clients and operators, review and migration; CI on every push.
      Same code
      This page runs a bundle built from that commit, the bundle a test in CI imports and checks. Nothing on it is a pasted figure.

      Four questions, four tests

      • Transactions and reconciliation. Every entry sums to zero, and the draft is checked against a month worked out by hand. month.test.ts
      • Several clients under one bottler. Statements filter the same postings; one client's operation leaves the others unchanged. month.test.ts
      • Migration. Quoted cells, conflicting rows and barrels already in stock are caught before anything is booked. migration.test.ts
      • Extracted data under review. Only the approved version is booked, in one step with the approval; an edit withdraws it. review.test.ts
